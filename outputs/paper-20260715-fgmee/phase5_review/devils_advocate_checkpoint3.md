# Devil's Advocate Report — Checkpoint 3

## Verdict: REVISE

## Critical Issues (Blocks Progression)

No critical issue is present in the research-stage report because it openly labels planned work and does not claim a completed physical result. It would become critical only if the same text were submitted as a completed research article without the missing experiments.

## Major Issues

### 1. The title borrows certainty from future experiments

- **Type:** Framing / Evidence.
- **Location:** Title and Abstract.
- **Problem:** “Independently validated” and “curvature--gradient interactions” read as achieved results, while the report says those experiments remain open.
- **Recommendation:** Rename the Stage 1 artifact as an evidence audit and protocol. Reserve the result title for the final manuscript after closure.

### 2. A null interaction could collapse the planned originality

- **Type:** Confirmation bias / Significance.
- **Location:** Sections 3.4, 4.4 and 6.
- **Problem:** The paper narrative still benefits from the hope that a non-additive interaction will appear.
- **Recommendation:** Freeze a publishable null-result path now: report uncertainty-bounded absence of interaction and cross-platform failure diagnostics without changing factors or outputs.

### 3. One-percent acceptance may be tradition, not validity

- **Type:** Appeal to precedent / Method.
- **Location:** Section 3.3.
- **Problem:** Prior tables motivate a small discrepancy, but 1% is not a universal correctness threshold.
- **Recommendation:** Treat 1% as an engineering target and report the full error budget. Do not label 1.01% “wrong” or 0.99% “correct.”

### 4. Publication bias exists inside simulation workflows

- **Type:** Survivorship bias.
- **Location:** Sections 3.5 and 5.3.
- **Problem:** Successful rows are easier to tabulate than memory failures, non-convergence and sign-inconsistent cases.
- **Recommendation:** Maintain a denominator of all attempted cases and publish exclusion/failure counts by reason.

## Minor Issues

- “Actual reasonableness” should be replaced by stated reference case and engineering range.
- The paper needs an authorship/credit plan for inherited code and predecessor contributions.
- The report should state that full-text acquisition can still change novelty wording.
